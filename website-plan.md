\# Personal Academic Website Plan



\## Goal

Build a personal academic website with a research showcase style.



\## Reference style

\- inspired by jedyang.com

\- cleaner and more modern than a plain faculty template

\- academic, professional, and visually structured

\- not startup-like



\## Owner

\- personal photo on homepage

\- academic personal website



\## Priority

Build the website structure first.

Do not invent detailed projects yet.

Use placeholder project cards that can be replaced later.



\## Required pages

\- Home

\- Research

\- Projects

\- Publications

\- Teaching

\- CV

\- Contact



\## Homepage sections

\- Hero section with personal photo

\- Short bio

\- Research themes

\- Featured projects (placeholder cards for now)

\- Selected publications

\- Teaching snapshot

\- Contact links



\## Style

\- modern academic

\- clean spacing

\- strong section hierarchy

\- card-based layout for projects

\- easy to update later
